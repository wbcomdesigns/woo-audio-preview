# A shopper plays the preview

## Steps
1. As a shopper, open a product with a preview: the player renders near add-to-cart and plays, pauses and seeks.
2. A product with multiple previews shows them all; a product with none shows no player.
3. Pro: the watermark plays over the track; the archive badge shows on the shop grid at the configured position.
4. 390px and dark, on Storefront / BuddyX / Reign / Twenty Twenty-Five: the player is usable, no overflow.

## Pass criteria
1. The preview plays on every theme and viewport; no console errors.
