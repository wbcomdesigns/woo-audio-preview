# Frequently asked questions

## What audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WMA, and WEBM. MP3 is recommended for the widest
cross-device compatibility. OGG does not play on iOS (iPhone and iPad), so use
MP3 if your audience is mainly on iOS.

## How many previews can I add per product?

Up to 3 in the free version. For more (albums, full chapter lists, sound packs)
the Pro add-on offers unlimited previews with dynamic add/remove.

## Can I use files hosted on Google Drive, SoundCloud, or Dropbox?

Yes. Google Drive, SoundCloud, and Dropbox links are recognised and played with
the right method for each. Amazon S3 and CloudFront direct URLs also work. Paste
the sharing URL into the URL field; the file must be publicly accessible.

## Will the player match my theme?

Yes. It uses a neutral design that inherits your theme's colours, and follows
WordPress enqueuing best practices. RTL stylesheets are included.

## Is the player mobile-friendly?

Yes. It is responsive with touch-friendly play/pause and progress controls, and
works on iOS, Android, and desktop browsers.

## What is the difference between free and Pro?

Free: up to 3 previews per product, all supported formats, cloud/CDN URL
support, responsive player, Media Library upload.

Pro adds: unlimited previews, dynamic add/remove, multi-vendor support (Dokan,
WCFM, WC Vendors, WC Marketplace), watermarking and voice-over protection,
preview duration control, custom player themes, and bulk import, plus priority
support.

## Does it work with multi-vendor marketplaces like Dokan?

Multi-vendor support is a Pro feature. The free plugin has no vendor-dashboard
integration.

## How can I stop my audio being downloaded?

In the free version, use short clips (30 to 60 seconds), upload lower-quality
preview files, and prefer streaming services like SoundCloud that limit direct
downloads. Advanced protection (watermarking, voice-over, time-limited previews)
is a Pro feature.

## Can I customise the player's appearance?

In the free version the player adapts to your theme automatically; there are no
per-player style options. Custom player themes and colour schemes are a Pro
feature.

## Are there developer hooks?

Yes, 14 filters and actions, including the Free-to-Pro seam filters, the meta
box hooks, and the front-end render hooks. See the Developer Guide.

## Where can I get support?

Free support is on the WordPress.org support forum for the plugin. Pro users get
priority email support.
